Item 1. Business

SampleCo, Inc. ("SampleCo", "we", "our", or "the Company") is a
diversified technology company that designs, develops, and sells consumer
electronics, software, online services, and cloud computing products.
Our principal product lines include the SampleBook personal computer,
the SamplePhone smartphone, the SampleTab tablet, and a growing portfolio
of subscription services including SampleCloud and SampleStream.

The Company was incorporated in Delaware in 1998 and is headquartered in
Cupertino, California. As of December 31, 2023, the Company had
approximately 154,000 full-time equivalent employees worldwide.

Our business strategy leverages our unique ability to design and develop
our own operating systems, hardware, application software, and services
to provide our customers products and solutions with innovative design,
superior ease-of-use, and seamless integration.

Item 1A. Risk Factors

The Company's business, financial condition, operating results, and
stock price can be affected by a number of factors, whether currently
known or unknown, including but not limited to those described below,
any one or more of which could, directly or indirectly, cause the
Company's actual financial condition and operating results to vary
materially from past, or from anticipated future, financial condition
and operating results.

Macroeconomic and Geopolitical Risks

The Company's operations and performance depend significantly on global
and regional economic conditions and adverse macroeconomic conditions,
including inflation, slower growth or recession, tightening of credit
markets, higher interest rates, sovereign debt crises, and currency
fluctuations, can materially adversely affect demand for the Company's
products and services.

Global markets for the Company's products and services are highly
competitive and subject to rapid technological change, and the Company
may be unable to compete effectively in these markets. Substantially
all of the Company's hardware products contain components and
sub-assemblies sourced from suppliers in Asia, particularly China.

Supply Chain and Manufacturing Risks

The Company depends on component and product manufacturing and logistical
services provided by outsourcing partners, many of which are located
outside of the United States. Substantially all of the Company's
manufacturing is performed in whole or in part by a few outsourcing
partners located primarily in China, India, Japan, South Korea, Taiwan,
and Vietnam. A significant concentration of this manufacturing is
currently performed by a small number of outsourcing partners, often in
single locations.

The Company has invested in increasing the resilience of its supply chain,
including diversifying manufacturing across multiple countries. However,
disruptions in transportation, public health crises, natural disasters,
political conflict, or other events could materially adversely affect
the Company's ability to deliver products to its customers on time.

Cybersecurity and Data Privacy Risks

The Company is subject to complex and evolving laws and regulations
worldwide regarding privacy, data protection, cybersecurity, and consumer
protection. Failure to comply with these laws and regulations could
result in significant fines, penalties, and reputational harm. The
Company has experienced and may experience in the future cybersecurity
incidents that could compromise the integrity of its products, services,
and data.

Artificial Intelligence Risks

The Company is increasingly incorporating artificial intelligence ("AI")
features into its products and services. The development and deployment
of AI technologies involves significant risks, including potential
inaccuracies in AI-generated outputs, biases in training data, evolving
regulatory frameworks, and intellectual property concerns. Failure to
manage these risks effectively could result in legal liability, harm to
the Company's reputation, or competitive disadvantage.

Item 2. Properties

The Company's headquarters is located in Cupertino, California. As of
December 31, 2023, the Company owned or leased approximately 26.0
million square feet of building space, primarily in the United States,
and to a lesser extent, in Asia and Europe. The Company believes its
existing facilities and equipment are in good operating condition and
are suitable for the conduct of its business.

Item 3. Legal Proceedings

The Company is subject to various legal proceedings and claims that
have arisen in the ordinary course of business and have not been fully
adjudicated.

Antitrust Litigation. In 2022, several plaintiffs filed lawsuits alleging
that the Company's distribution practices for the SampleStore digital
marketplace violate antitrust laws. The cases are in early stages and
the Company intends to defend against these claims vigorously.

Patent Infringement. The Company is the defendant in multiple patent
infringement suits, including a suit filed in 2023 alleging that the
Company's SamplePhone infringes on certain wireless communication
patents. The Company believes the claims are without merit.

Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and
Results of Operations

The following discussion should be read in conjunction with the
consolidated financial statements and the related notes that appear
elsewhere in this Annual Report on Form 10-K.

Fiscal 2023 Highlights

Net sales for fiscal 2023 increased 4% or $14.7 billion compared to
fiscal 2022, primarily due to higher Services net sales, partially
offset by lower iPhone-equivalent net sales. Services net sales grew
9% year-over-year, driven by record performance across SampleCloud,
SampleStream, and the App Store.

Gross margin was 44.1% in fiscal 2023, compared to 43.3% in fiscal 2022,
reflecting a favorable shift toward higher-margin Services revenue and
operational efficiencies in manufacturing.

Operating expenses increased 6% year-over-year, driven by higher
research and development spending on artificial intelligence, custom
silicon, and new product categories.

Liquidity and Capital Resources

The Company believes its existing balances of cash, cash equivalents,
and marketable securities, together with cash generated from operations,
will be sufficient to satisfy its working capital needs, capital asset
purchases, dividends, share repurchases, debt repayments, and other
liquidity requirements associated with its existing operations over
the next 12 months and beyond.

As of December 31, 2023, the Company held $162.1 billion in cash, cash
equivalents, and marketable securities. The Company returned $99.5
billion to shareholders during fiscal 2023 through share repurchases
and dividend payments.

Item 7A. Quantitative and Qualitative Disclosures About Market Risk
